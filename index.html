Save enhanced copies

Saved for next time

Cropper balances likely neutral areas, improves contrast, and gently lifts faded colour. After a copy saves successfully, its source moves into a Pre-Enhancement subfolder.

How does it decide?

This is an automatic, rule-based correction. Each photo is measured separately. Cropper balances likely whites and darker areas independently to reduce yellow or red casts. For faded prints it restores the range between dull whites and grey blacks while keeping highlight texture. Very cloudy, nearly neutral prints get extra gray-veil and local-contrast correction. Muted colours get a small lift that avoids already vivid areas. It skips corrections that do not appear needed. Review the preview, especially for intentionally warm or sepia photos. It cannot reconstruct missing facial detail.
