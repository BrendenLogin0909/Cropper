Cropper balances likely neutral areas, improves contrast, and gently lifts faded colour. Each photo gets its own correction.

How does it decide?

Each photo is measured separately. Cropper looks for likely whites and greys to reduce a colour cast, widens a narrow light-to-dark range, and gently lifts weak colour when present. It skips corrections that do not appear needed. Review the before-and-after preview, especially for intentionally warm or sepia photos.
